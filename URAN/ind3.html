Company's History

Founded in 2022, Uran Construction quickly established itself as a trusted name in the construction and infrastructure sector. Starting with small residential projects, the company built a reputation for quality craftsmanship, attention to detail, and reliability. Over the years, Uran Construction expanded its services to include commercial developments, large-scale infrastructure projects, and innovative building solutions. With a focus on sustainability and modern engineering practices, Uran Construction has grown into a respected industry leader, delivering projects that shape communities and drive progress.
